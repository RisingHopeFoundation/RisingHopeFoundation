Our Work

Rising Hope focuses on practical ways to support people facing hardship in our communities.

AREA OUR FOCUS
Food Support Community food initiatives and food parcels for people experiencing food insecurity.
Community Care Identifying local needs and connecting people with practical support.
Partnerships Working with responsible organisations, businesses, churches and community members.
Future Opportunities Exploring ways to help communities become more resilient and self-sufficient.

Why Food Support?

Food insecurity can affect a person's ability to focus on school, work, family and daily life. Providing food support is one practical way to meet an immediate need while building stronger community connections.

Our Approach

  1. Listen to community needs.
  2. Identify practical ways to help.
  3. Work with trusted partners where possible.
  4. Use resources responsibly.
  5. Learn from each project and improve.

Small acts can create meaningful change.
