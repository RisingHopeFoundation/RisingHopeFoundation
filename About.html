About Rising Hope

Rising Hope Foundation is a community-focused initiative built around one simple belief: people should not have to face hardship alone.

We aim to respond to practical community needs, beginning with food insecurity and support for people experiencing difficult circumstances.

Our Mission

To provide practical assistance, encourage dignity and create opportunities for people and communities to move forward.

Our Vision

A community where people have access to support, care and opportunities to build a more secure future.

Our Values

VALUE WHAT IT MEANS
Dignity Every person deserves to be treated with respect.
Compassion We respond to people with care and understanding.
Integrity We aim to act responsibly and honestly.
Community We believe lasting change is stronger when people work together.

Our purpose is bigger than one person.

Together, we can help create communities where hope has room to grow.
